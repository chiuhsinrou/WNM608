News

CHICAGO, April 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Today Athena Chiu is pleased to announce a collaboration with acclaimed designer Tiffany Howell and actor Laura Harrier. The 87 piece collection features products in categories across the home including furniture, lighting, textiles and decor. The assortment of the collection balances elegance and surprise, transforming sculpture into dramatic statements of interior glamour.

The friendship turned creative partnership between founder of design studio Night Palm Tiffany Howell and Hollywood darling Laura Harrier is grounded in their affinity for vintage cinema and iconic fashion. This poignant expression of a shared design language was clearly translated into the modern heritage essence of Athena Chiu's quality product.
